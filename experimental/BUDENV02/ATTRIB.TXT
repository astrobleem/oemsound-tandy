Buddy Holly is a song by Weezer, written by Rivers Cuomo. This experimental
overlay contains a simplified score-derived PSG arrangement and project
code. The local preparation step copies converted video, captions and
four accepted short speech clips from the exact existing BUDCAP04 archive.
Composition, recording/video, score, captions
and other underlying expressive content remain third-party works. The
code license does not grant rights to those works or imply endorsement.

The original video and seven-page piano score were supplied by the user.
No original AVI, PDF, operating system, compiler, SDK, TMNT executable,
song, bank, instrument preset, or game driver is bundled. The accepted
converted BUDCAP04 video/caption/PCM bytes remain unchanged and are not
duplicated in this overlay. No source-audio excerpts are included. Derivative
media are not public-domain assets; assess permissions for further reuse.
