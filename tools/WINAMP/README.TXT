TANDY WINAMP

Extract the TWXT2 folder and run SETUP.EXE from DOS to install. Then run C:\WINAMP\GO.

The native installer supplies one shared SOUND.DRV and all required
player DLLs and launch helpers. INSTALL.TXT describes normal use, repair and
removal. SOURCE and EVIDENCE contain corresponding source and development
records; they are not consumer preparation steps.

The player offers Default and Chamber FauxBlaster MIDI instrument banks.
Click the bank button to change it; your choice is saved automatically.
