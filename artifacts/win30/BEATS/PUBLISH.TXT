Tandy Beats Lab publication layout

Editable source is in src/win30/BEATS. The native executable, paired driver,
screenshot, native emulator audio, and all sealed evidence are here.
MILESTONE.ZIP preserves the complete, unchanged standalone package.
MANIFEST.JSON describes that package's original paths, including SOURCE/.
The original README's no-publication sentence records the earlier snapshot.

Build from the repository root with your existing licensed DOS toolchain:
python3 src/win30/BEATS/BUILD.PY --repo . --dosbox <DOSBox-X> --out <new-directory>
No compiler, Windows, DDK, or guest files are added by this publication.

BEATS calls the paired experimental MIDIMAP.DRV directly. This is not a
system-wide MIDI runtime, midiOut device, or standard MIDI-file player.
Do not replace or register a system MIDIMAP.DRV. Accepted SOUND work is unchanged.
Stop, normal close, and cooperative SOUND ownership have native test evidence.
Abrupt termination, physical Tandy hardware, and manual CUA remain unqualified.
