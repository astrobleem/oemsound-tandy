WEEZER.WZV SHA256 05914fe661ae993c73930c175a5f1da6de2a38938f58af6081788be4ae622312
This is the 966-frame, 241.4-second tiny RGBI encoding used in Chad's
approved colorful CGA recording. It differs in palette quantization from
the historical WINPLAY.ZIP WZV (01ccbf0d...0b6bcc9), which is retained.
No replacement video content, desktop overlay, or sampled soundtrack is added.

WEEZER.WZP is a lossless plane-order transform of this WZV; use host/PLANAR.PY.
WEEZER.WZM and WEEZER.CUE are byte-identical to the historical WINPLAY.ZIP.
SPEAKER.WZM is the accepted sparse one-voice arrangement. Its event list is
score/SPEAKER.JSON and its deterministic written-score generator is
host/SPEAKER.PY. It uses 667 timed states; services add a final silent state.
CGA16.BMP is the original 16-color neon harbor wallpaper made for this demo.

See ../README.TXT and ../../ATTRIB.TXT for usage and attribution.
