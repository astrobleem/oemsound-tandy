Final exclusive-end and cleanup regression checks
Final BUILD06 refuses music service at/after cueEnd, and checks cueEnd
again after a video read before drawing. It keeps the DOS critical-error
FAIL handler active through logging, then restores the prior vectors.
The exact final binary completed the full-length test above, plus:
- Synthetic cue ending at exactly 750 ms with an active note-on there:
  six earlier states applied, seven records read ahead, and the exact-end
  state was not applied. End result: mode 3 and mute mask 0.
- Mid-play video file truncated by the host: error 15, mode 3 and mute 0.
Supplementary prior-build checks covered the same boundary at 64/1000,
Escape and Space stop, out-of-order timestamps, out-of-range pitch,
malformed video length and invalid cue range. All tested failures stopped
cleanly. The full conservative song comparison used BUILD04; BUILD05/06
only tightened terminal checks and kept the FAIL handler through logging.
Real removable-media critical errors and launching from inside actual
Windows were not induced; their guards are present in the final source.
