A user-supplied seven-page Buddy Holly piano score informed the arrangement.
The original WINPLAY and DOSPLAY packages contain converted frames and a
PSG arrangement, without sampled soundtrack or subtitles. The later
BUDCAP04 package also contains a full-video subtitle track and four short
sampled dialogue excerpts (10.60 seconds total); it does not play the
original recorded song vocals. Those words and recordings remain
third-party copyrighted material. No original AVI, full soundtrack WAV,
score PDF, OS, compiler or SDK is added by BUDCAP04.
